- Kenney City Kit (Suburban): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban
- Kenney City Kit (Roads): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
- Kenney Nature Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Kenney Platformer Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-kit
- Kenney Blaster Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit

Kenney identifies its free asset-page packs as CC0. Recheck each pack's live licence before shipping. Import model files into the project; do not hotlink third-party assets at runtime.
